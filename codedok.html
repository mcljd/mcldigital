The story

Built in Cushendall by people who build boats.

I work at Redbay Boats on the Antrim Coast — one of the UK's best-regarded RIB builders. Every week I watched new Stormforce owners drive away with a brilliant boat and a manila folder of certificates they had no system to manage. Six months later, the folder was wet, half the certs were expired, and they were calling the surveyor in a panic.

I partnered with Joe McCollam — an MCA-recognised naval architect and marine surveyor who surveys vessels across NI and the Irish Sea. Every SMS template in CodedOK has been tested against Joe's real surveyor standard. When he says a document is survey-ready, it is.

The vision is simple: every UK and Irish coded vessel should run compliance the way Amazon runs delivery — tracked, reminded, and evidenced without anyone having to think about it.

"Before CodedOK, I'd arrive on site and spend 40 minutes with the skipper finding things. Now I open the link on my iPad before I leave the car. Everything is there — certificates, crew tickets, service records, code citations. It saves me at least two hours per survey, and the operator isn't embarrassed when something's missing."

FAQ

Questions we get asked.

Is this MCA-approved?

CodedOK is independent of the MCA. We're not affiliated with them and we don't claim to be. What we do is help you manage your compliance evidence and deadlines to the standards set by the MCA Sport & Pleasure Vessel Code 2025 and Workboat Code Edition 3. The paperwork still has to meet the standard — we help you get there and stay there.

Will my surveyor accept it?

If they're a CodedOK surveyor, yes — you can share your pack directly in-app. If they're not, you can export a PDF inspection pack and send it by email. The document is the same; only the delivery changes. Our launch surveyor — McCollam Marine — is MCA-recognised and helped build every checklist template in the app.

What if I'm offline at sea?

Your vessel profile and certificate records are available offline on your phone once loaded. Expiry dates and reminders work without signal. You can photograph certs for later upload when you're back in range. The inspection pack generates offline and uploads when you reconnect.

What if I sell the boat?

You own your data. Transfer the vessel to the new owner's account, or export everything as a PDF evidence pack — service history, certificates, survey reports, SMS — and hand it over as part of the sale. A comprehensive digital record adds value at point of sale.

How does it work with my insurance?

Your compliance evidence — every certificate, service record and surveyor sign-off — is available as a single PDF pack if you ever need to make a claim. We never share your data with insurers, harbour authorities, or any third party without your explicit permission. You see exactly what's shared, when, and why.

Can I export everything?

Yes. Every certificate, survey record, crew qualification, deficiency log, and your AI-generated SMS can be exported as PDF at any time. If you cancel, you export everything first. We don't hold your data hostage — your data goes home with you.

Who built this?

Joseph McLaughlin — based in Cushendall, Co. Antrim. Works at Redbay Boats. Built CodedOK after watching every customer struggle with the same paperwork problem. Partnered with Joe McCollam (McCollam Marine, naval architect and MCA-recognised surveyor) who tested every template and checklist against his real survey standard.

Is my data shared with anyone?

No. Your vessel data is never shared with the MCA, insurers, harbour authorities, or any third party without your explicit opt-in. You control what's shared and with whom. If you share a pack with your surveyor, they see exactly what you send — nothing else. You can revoke access any time.
