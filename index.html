FAQ

Questions we get asked.

Straight answers. No hedging.

How is this only £150?
No agency overhead, no project managers, no "we'll have a designer on it next week". The AI builder does the heavy lifting on the first draft; we polish what comes out. Five days, fixed price, done.
How long does it take?
5 working days for a Just-the-site project. Bigger custom builds (apps, dashboards, AI tools) range from 1–6 weeks depending on scope. Every quote includes a fixed delivery date.
Do I own the site and the domain?
Yes. Full source code, domain registered in your name, deployment access yours. No vendor lock-in. You can move hosts or designers any time.
What if I want changes after it's live?
First two weeks of small changes are free. After that, £30/hr or a £15/mo retainer if you want ongoing tweaks. No 12-month contracts.
Will my site rank on Google?
Yes — every site we ship has proper meta tags, mobile-first responsive design, structured data and a sitemap. You'll need to claim your Google Business Profile too (we'll walk you through it free).
Are you GDPR compliant?
Yes. Minimal data collection, encrypted in transit and at rest, clear data export and deletion paths. Contact forms only collect what's needed.
