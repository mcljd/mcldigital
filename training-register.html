Training Register.

A compliance app that tells a warehouse team who is trained, who is overdue, and who is coming due next.

Client typeDistribution StackPython HostingOn premise StatusLive
The problem

They were running it on a spreadsheet.

The site was tracking training on a shared Excel matrix. Nobody trusted it. Rows got overwritten. Dates were missed. You could not tell if a forklift driver was still current without opening the file and reading across seventeen columns of half updated data.

Renewals were slipping. Auditors were asking for evidence that took a day to pull together. The team had outgrown the sheet.

What it does

The whole site, one screen.

  • Live training matrixThe whole site in one view. Filter by team.
  • Red, amber and greenExpiry tracking with clear status, so renewals get planned instead of missed.
  • New starter flagsAnyone new is flagged until their onboarding training is done.
  • Role based trainingA forklift driver and an office worker see different training. Mandatory items follow the job.
  • Leavers archivedPeople are archived, never deleted, so audit history stays intact.
  • Certificates on the recordPhotograph the paper cert on a phone, attach it, done.
  • ReportsTraining gaps, upcoming renewals, and total training spend in cost and hours.
  • Three access levelsAdmin, team manager and worker. People only see what they should.
  • Full audit logEvery change tracked, with who made it and when.
  • Desktop and phoneSame app on any screen.

How it was built

Simple stack. Stays on their network.

Built as a Python web app that runs on the company's own Windows server, so all data stays inside their network. It has secure logins, protection against the usual web attacks, nightly backups and Microsoft sign in. An automated test suite runs on every change so updates go live without breaking things.
