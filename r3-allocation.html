R3 Allocation.

A stock allocation app for a multi store fashion retailer. It tells merchandising what to move where, and what to stop reordering.

Client typeFashion retail StackWeb app UsersHead office + store StatusLive
The problem

Stock in the wrong shop.

The team was moving stock between shops on gut feel and end of week emails. Best sellers sat in the wrong shop for weeks. Dead sizes clogged shelves for a full season. Nobody could pull a proper list of what to transfer without cross checking three spreadsheets.

They needed one place that read the numbers and said, plainly, what to do about them.

What it does

One place. Every store.

  • Weekly transfer suggestionsEvery store, ranked and reasoned. Each row says why the move matters.
  • Slow mover flagsItems that have stopped selling and need to move or mark down.
  • Dead size range detectionSizes that never sell in a given shop, so buyers stop reordering them.
  • Combined reportTransfers, slow movers and dead sizes stitched into one export, per store and site wide.
  • Rich filtersBrand, department, sub category, season and gender.
  • Rejection logBuyers can override. The tool learns what to skip next run.
  • Role based accessStore, admin and support console see different things.
  • Exports built inCSV and ZIP. One click sends the report straight to every store manager.

How it was built

Runs inside their network.

A web app that runs inside the retailer's own network. It reads sales and stock data from their existing systems, runs the allocation logic in the background, and has the reports ready for the merchandising team to review each morning. Secure logins, role based access and a full audit trail.
